About JPH

I'm Justine, a professional photo editor based in Cebu, Philippines, specializing in real estate, architectural and portrait retouching.

With over 3 years of professional editing experience and a background in Information Technology, I combine technical precision with a strong eye for composition, color and detail.

My goal is simple: to transform raw photographs into clean, polished and professional images that help photographers, businesses and creatives present their work at its best.
